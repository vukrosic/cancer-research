# Claim and evidence policy

| Tier | Allowed statement | Minimum evidence |
|---|---|---|
| T0 | Pipeline executes and reproduces | Locked inputs, hashes, tests, rerun |
| T1 | Association in named public screens | Effect size, uncertainty, controls |
| T2 | Claim replicates across a held-out source | Frozen rule, independent screen family |
| T3 | Biological vulnerability hypothesis | T2 plus artifact review and mechanistic rationale |
| T4 | Clinical relevance or utility | Out of scope without clinical validation |

Results from this repository can nominate experimental priorities. They cannot show
that inhibiting a gene is safe, druggable, tumour-selective, effective in patients,
or superior to existing care.

Every result card must state its tier. Exploratory analyses remain exploratory until
they are converted into a new preregistered experiment with untouched test data.
