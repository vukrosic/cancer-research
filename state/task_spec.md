# Durable task specification

## Goal

Create a reusable computational cancer research program that identifies which genetic
vulnerability claims replicate across independent CRISPR screen families and deserve
orthogonal experimental validation.

## Current milestone

Preserve EXP-015 as a feasibility-only, non-confirmatory ARID1A/ARID1B result
and release EXP-016 only as a protocol-deviation/non-confirmatory
ARID1A/KEAP1 record. The EXP-016 planning-power mismatch is documented and
must not be relabeled as a clean preregistered or confirmatory result. Select
the next distinct outcome-blind dependency direction after the release. Do not
pool Avana and KY, rescue failed lineages with post hoc exclusions, or change
thresholds to upgrade either experiment. Keep the EXP-014 boundary immutable.

## Program success

A public, one-command reproducible benchmark; a frozen candidate ledger; positive
controls; artifact-aware independent replication; preserved null results; and claim
cards that another AI or human can continue without conversation history.

## Limits

Public non-identifiable data, <=12 GB storage, 16 GB Mac compatibility, no clinical
claims, and no actionability claims without orthogonal experiments.
