## Claim decision

EXP034 is released only as a T1 descriptive feasibility result. Avana was
nominally negative and statistically directional but failed the effect-size
and no-positive-lineage requirements. KY was positive, uncertain in the
preregistered direction, and heterogeneous. The frozen data do not support a
robust TP53-proxy–TDG dependency transport claim.

The source hypothesis concerned p53-deficient-cancer/TDG synthetic lethality
reported in the literature ([Nature Chemical Biology study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41589-025-02100-1)).
EXP034 tests only a damaging-matrix TP53 proxy against genetic TDG knockout
dependency. It does not establish functional TP53 loss, TDG-inhibitor
sensitivity, DNA-repair mechanism, causal synthetic lethality, pharmacologic
response, treatment benefit, patient selection, clinical utility, or a
confirmatory claim.
