  replication, not a novelty claim.
- Avana and KY are source-specific corroboration cohorts from one release; raw
  score magnitudes are never compared across sources and no biological
  independence is claimed.
- The damaging matrix and eligible screen/model context are loaded and recorded
  before either target-gene endpoint column is opened.
- Lineage-stratified matrix-status-versus-intact comparisons prevent a pooled lineage
  composition contrast from becoming the primary result.
- The primary STAG2 pair and secondary PDS5B pair are frozen before target-score
  access; the secondary is descriptive only and cannot rescue the primary.
- Exact source×pair×lineage×status counts and a pre-outcome 80%-power design
  sensitivity are required before target access; KY's small STAG2 status group
  may force a feasibility-only interpretation.
- Endpoint completeness is a sealed gate after context ledger publication and
  before any group summary or contrast.
- The lower-tail permutation, fixed pair-count estimand, bootstrap, six gates,
  and no-rescue claim boundary are frozen in the preregistration.

No target-gene score, dependency contrast, p-value, interval, or result has been
