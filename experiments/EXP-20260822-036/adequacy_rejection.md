# EXP036 outcome-free adequacy rejection

## Candidate

The candidate was RB1 damaging proxy → SKP2 dependency, motivated by the
primary [Nature Genetics study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41588-025-02108-2),
whose Supplementary Table 3 lists SKP2 among genes reproduced in an isogenic
RB1 screen.

## Frozen audit

The frozen metadata join found:

- Avana: 57 RB1-damaging, 918 matrix-intact models, 13 mixed lineages;
- KY: 18 RB1-damaging, 297 matrix-intact models, 9 mixed lineages.

The protocol requires at least 20 exposed models per source. KY therefore
fails the T0 context-adequacy gate. No planning power, canonical roster,
endpoint score, rank outcome, or inference was computed. The gate was not
weakened to make the candidate executable.

## Decision

`REJECTED_BEFORE_ENDPOINT_T0_CONTEXT_ADEQUACY`.

This does not test or refute RB1/SKP2 biology. It records that the frozen
DepMap 23Q4 Avana/KY cohorts cannot support this repository's preregistered
two-source transport design.
